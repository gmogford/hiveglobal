- **Bull:** One of very few tokens where fees measurably become buy pressure. Revenue is diversifying into RWA/equity perps (52% of volume in mid-July, [REPORTED]). ETF access is a structural bid.
- **Bear (argued fully):** At $20.9B and a ~3.9–7% buyback yield, HYPE is priced like a mature exchange, not a mispriced one. Revenue tracks perp volume, which tracks volatility and leverage. A leverage unwind cuts revenue and price together. The 77% locked supply is a permanent overhang, and slow claiming just defers it. The +22% week is momentum, not a fundamental revaluation.
- **Invalidation:** 30-day fees fall >40% from the August run-rate; the buyback is paused or redirected; unlocked supply claims jump materially (team selling); a major exploit or liquidation-engine failure.
- **Confirmed catalyst:** none dated. **Speculation:** "RWA perps become 75% of volume by 2027".
- **Verdict:** Organic, fee-backed value accrual; the edge is **not** "cheap". It is traded two-sided on short horizons because it is the highest-beta liquid name with a real flow story.

- **Verdict:** The strongest value-accrual mismatch *if* revenue holds. The system trades it two-sided because the revenue claim cannot be verified from here.
